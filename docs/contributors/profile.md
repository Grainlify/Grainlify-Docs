# Your Profile

*Last updated: 16 August 2026*

Your profile is your public track record on Grainlify — anyone can view it,
whether they're a maintainer deciding who to assign an issue to or another
contributor checking out the leaderboard.

## What's on it

- **Contribution calendar** — a heatmap of your activity over the last year,
  the same style as GitHub's own contribution graph.
- **Contribution history** — every issue and pull request you've worked on,
  grouped by month.
- **Projects** — what you've contributed to, and any projects you maintain
  yourself.
- **Languages and ecosystems** — a summary of what you work in most.
- **Rank badge** — your current tier, based on your
  **[leaderboard position](./ranks-and-leaderboard.md)**.
- **Verified badge** — shown if you've completed
  **[identity verification](./verifying-your-identity.md)**, as a trust
  signal to others.

## Editing your profile

Go to **Settings → Profile** to update your bio, website, and social links
(Telegram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, X, Discord). These show up on your public
profile page alongside your GitHub identity.

## Viewing someone else's profile

Every contributor's profile is public — open it from the leaderboard, from
a project's contributor list, or by visiting their profile URL directly.
It's a quick way to see someone's track record before teaming up on an
issue or deciding who to bring onto a project.
