# Getting Started as a Contributor

Grainlify connects you with open-source projects that want contributors, and
keeps track of what you've contributed so it counts for something — a public
track record, a spot on the leaderboard, and rewards for helping grow the
community.

## Create your account

There's one way in: **Sign in with GitHub.**

1. Go to [grainlify.com](https://grainlify.com) and click **Sign In**.
2. Authorize Grainlify on GitHub. We only ever read your public profile and
   public activity to match you with projects — we never touch private
   repositories without you explicitly connecting one yourself.
3. You're in. Your account is linked to your GitHub identity, so your avatar,
   username, and public contributions show up automatically.

There's no separate signup form and nothing to fill in up front — your first
sign-in *is* your account.

## The basic loop

Once you're signed in, using Grainlify comes down to four things:

1. **[Find a project or issue](./finding-work.md)** you want to work on, using
   Discover or Browse.
2. **[Apply to an issue](./applying-to-issues.md)** and, once a maintainer
   assigns it to you, do the work as a normal GitHub pull request.
3. Every issue and pull request you complete adds to your public contribution
   history, which is what your **[rank and leaderboard](./ranks-and-leaderboard.md)**
   position are built from.
4. Separately, **[invite people](./referrals.md)** and
   **[follow us on social media](./social-follow.md)** to earn points you can
   **[redeem for USDC](./rewards-and-redemptions.md)**.

## Your profile

Everything you do is reflected on your **[profile page](./profile.md)** —
a public page anyone can view, showing your contribution history, the
projects you've worked on, and your current rank.

## Staying in the loop

Turn on **[notifications](./notifications.md)** so you hear about
application decisions, merged pull requests, and reward payouts without
having to keep checking the dashboard.
