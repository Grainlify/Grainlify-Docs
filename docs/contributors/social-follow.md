# Social Follow

*Last updated: 16 August 2026*

Following Grainlify on **LinkedIn and X** is **required to be eligible** for
the Founding Contributor Pool. It earns nothing by itself — it's a
requirement, not a payment.

:::note Looking for points, or for Telegram?
This changed twice: following stopped paying, and the platforms are no longer
GitHub, Telegram and LinkedIn. See
**[What changed](../what-changed.md)**.
:::

## What you need to do

1. Go to **Settings → Rewards**.
2. Follow Grainlify on both platforms:
   - **LinkedIn** — [linkedin.com/company/grainlify](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grainlify)
   - **X** — [x.com/grainlify](https://x.com/grainlify)
3. Take a screenshot for each showing you're following.
4. Choose both screenshots, then **submit them together**.

## Both are submitted together

You choose a screenshot for each platform and submit once. There's no way to
submit one on its own, and that's deliberate: a submission that's half
approved has no clear answer to "is this person eligible?", and it would leave
a reviewer making two decisions that only make sense together.

Your submission is then **approved or rejected as a whole**.

## If your proof is rejected

You'll see the reason. Fix what it says, choose two new screenshots, and
submit again — there's no limit on attempts.

## Approval can be withdrawn

An approval isn't permanent. If it's withdrawn you'll see that it happened,
**and why**, on the same page — and you can follow again and submit new
screenshots to become eligible.

:::note What we actually check, stated precisely
Eligibility is confirmed when the pool is shared out. What that check does is
confirm that **your approved follow has not been revoked**. It is *not* a live
re-check that you are still following on the day.

LinkedIn and X don't give us a way to verify a follow automatically, which is
why proof is a screenshot reviewed by a person in the first place — and that
same limitation applies at settlement. So unfollowing is not automatically
detected. We'd rather tell you that than imply a check we can't perform.
:::

## What makes a good screenshot

Make sure it clearly shows:

- The Grainlify account you're following
- That **your** account is the one following it — a visible "Following" button,
  or your profile in the followers list

## Why proof instead of automatic checks

Neither platform offers a way to confirm a follow automatically, so proof is
reviewed by a person. Reviews are usually quick, but during busy periods they
may take a little longer.
