# Notifications

Stay on top of everything happening with your applications, pull requests,
and rewards — without checking the dashboard constantly.

## What you'll be notified about

As a contributor:

| You'll hear from us when... |
|---|
| A maintainer assigns you to an issue you applied for |
| Your application to an issue isn't accepted |
| A pull request you opened gets merged |
| A referral you made is completed |
| Your social-follow proof is approved or rejected |

If you also maintain projects on Grainlify, you'll additionally hear about:

| You'll hear from us when... |
|---|
| A contributor applies to an issue on one of your projects |

## In-app and email

Every notification can reach you two ways:

- **In-app** — shows up in the bell icon in the top navigation, with an
  unread count so you never miss one.
- **Email** — sent to the email address on your account, so you know even
  when you're not on the site.

Both are **on by default**. You don't need to set anything up to start
receiving them.

## Turning notifications on or off

Go to **Settings → Notifications**. Each notification type has its own
switch for in-app and email, so you can, for example, keep in-app alerts on
for everything but only get emailed about the things that matter most to
you. Use **Enable all** / **Disable all** to change everything at once.
