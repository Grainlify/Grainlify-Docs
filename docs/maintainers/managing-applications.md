# Managing Applications

When a contributor wants to work on one of your issues, they apply right
from Grainlify — here's where you review that and decide who gets the work.

## Where to look

Open **Maintainers → Issues**. You'll see every issue across your connected
projects in one list, with search and filters for status (open/closed),
whether it has applicants, whether it's already assigned, and how long it's
been sitting without activity.

Click into any issue to see two tabs:

- **Applications** — everyone who's applied, with their message.
- **Discussions** — the full comment thread on the issue, in case there's
  other back-and-forth worth reading.

## Reviewing an applicant

Each application shows the contributor's profile, when they applied, and
their message about how they'd approach the issue. From here you can:

- **Assign** — makes them the official assignee on GitHub and lets them know
  they've got it.
- **Reject** — lets them know you're passing this time, so they can move on
  to something else. This doesn't remove their original application from
  the thread, it just adds your response.

If you already assigned someone and need to free the issue back up, use
**Unassign** — this removes them as assignee and reopens the issue to new
applications.

These are one-at-a-time decisions — there's no bulk-assign or bulk-reject,
so review each application on its own.

## Posting an announcement

You can also post a message directly to an issue from Grainlify — handy for
things like clarifying scope or letting several applicants know the issue's
been claimed, without leaving the dashboard.

## A tip on picking a contributor

Every applicant's message links back to their **[profile](../contributors/profile.md)**,
which shows their contribution history and rank — a quick way to gauge
experience before deciding who to assign.
