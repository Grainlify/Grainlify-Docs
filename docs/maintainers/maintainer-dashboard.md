# Your Maintainer Dashboard

*Last updated: 16 August 2026*

Open **Maintainers → Dashboard** for a single-screen overview of everything
happening across the projects you maintain — built specifically from your
own projects' issues and pull requests, not platform-wide numbers.

## What's on it

- **Stats cards** — at-a-glance counts across your connected projects (open
  issues, pending applications, and similar top-line numbers).
- **Recent activity feed** — a running list of what's just happened: new
  applications, new pull requests, and other project events, newest first.
- **Applications chart** — a visual read on application volume over time, so
  you can spot whether interest in your project is picking up or slowing
  down.

## Why check it regularly

It's the fastest way to catch a backlog before it grows — a spike in
pending applications on the dashboard is your cue to head over to
**[Managing Applications](./managing-applications.md)** and start reviewing,
rather than finding out days later that good contributors have been waiting
on a decision.
